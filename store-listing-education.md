# Store update: FactChecker Pro Education (version 1.6.0.0)

## How to submit

Yes, ship this as an update to the same Store listing. Do not create a new app.

1. Run `build-msix.bat` (or `npm run dist:appx`). The new file appears in `release/` as FactChecker Pro 1.6.0.appx. The version is 1.6.0, and the Store needs the fourth number to be 0, so it becomes 1.6.0.0. It is higher than everything submitted before.
2. In Partner Center open your app, then Start update, then Packages. Upload the new appx.
3. Under Store listings add Dutch (Netherlands) next to English. Paste the texts below. Replace the screenshots with the ones in `store-assets/screenshots` (files starting with en- for English, nl- for Dutch).
4. Update the age rating questionnaire. The app now handles classes and student work.
5. Update the privacy policy link. It must say what is stored (classes, student work, names, email) and, for Google Classroom, what is read and why. Google verification asks for the same.
6. Submit. Existing users get the update automatically.

## English

**Name:** FactChecker Pro Education

**Short description:** Teach and learn fact checking. Classes, assignments, evidence boards and feedback in one app.

**Description**

FactChecker Pro Education helps teachers and students check claims together. Pick your role and start in a minute.

Teachers create classes, set assignments from articles, images or claims, and review how students reached their conclusions. You score with your own rubric. Automatic indicators help you look, but they never decide a grade. Connect Google Classroom to import your class list, publish assignments and send reviewed scores back as drafts.

Students work step by step: find evidence for and against, judge each source, write a conclusion and say honestly whether AI was used. Short modules teach source evaluation, claim checking and more.

Journalists and researchers get an evidence board, a citation assistant and a clear record of how a claim was checked.

Available in English and Dutch. Your data stays yours, and you can export or delete it at any time.

**What's new in this version**

A new modern look with smoother buttons and clearer text. Full Dutch language support. Connect Google Classroom to import classes and send reviewed scores. Teams and Canvas follow later.

**Features**

- Classes with join codes and class lists
- Assignments from articles, images and claims
- Evidence board with source ratings
- Teacher rubric review and feedback
- Learning modules on source evaluation and claim checking
- Honest AI use disclosure
- Citation assistant
- Google Classroom import, publish and draft grades
- English and Dutch
- Export and delete your data

**Search terms:** fact check, education, media literacy, classroom, teacher, students, sources, misinformation, journalism, research
